Why CraftProfitCalc exists

Many Etsy sellers make sales without fully understanding what they keep after fees, materials, shipping, packaging, discounts, and ads. CraftProfitCalc exists to make pricing decisions clearer before sellers spend time producing and fulfilling orders.

Who we help

We help handmade sellers, digital product creators, sticker shops, jewelry sellers, candle makers, print-on-demand sellers, and other Etsy shop owners who need simple, practical pricing math.

How our calculators work

RevenueWe start with selling price and shipping charged to the customer.
CostsWe include product cost, shipping cost, packaging, and ad cost where relevant.
FeesWe estimate listing, transaction, processing, and optional offsite ad fees.

Editorial approach

Our guides are written to be practical, formula-based, and connected to tools sellers can actually use. We avoid pretending there is one perfect margin for every shop because Etsy product economics vary widely.

Disclaimer

CraftProfitCalc is not affiliated with Etsy. Our tools provide estimates only. Sellers should verify fees, taxes, and final pricing decisions with their own data and official sources.
