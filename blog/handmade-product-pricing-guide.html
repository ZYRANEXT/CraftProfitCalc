Common Etsy pricing mistakes

  • Forgetting shipping supplies
  • Treating free shipping as free
  • Ignoring transaction fees on shipping
  • Not accounting for refunds or replacements
  • Using discounts without recalculating profit

FAQ

How do I calculate Etsy profit?

Use the profit formula above or enter your numbers into CraftProfitCalc.

What is a good profit margin for Etsy?

The right margin depends on product type, cost structure, labor time, and whether the product is handmade, digital, custom, or print-on-demand.

Should I offer free shipping on Etsy?

Free shipping can work, but the cost still needs to be included somewhere in your pricing.

Do Etsy fees include shipping?

Explain that sellers should consider fees, shipping charged, and shipping cost when calculating real profit.

Is an Etsy profit calculator better than a spreadsheet?

A calculator is faster for quick checks. A spreadsheet can be useful for tracking many products over time.

Conclusion

The goal is not just to make more sales. The goal is to make sales that actually make money.
